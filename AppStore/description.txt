Chore Wheel keeps a household's recurring chores on your iPhone, without an account, a subscription, or a cloud server.

Set up your household: add local named profiles, then add recurring chores with an estimated effort and due interval. Chore Wheel selects the next eligible person by least completed-plus-open assigned effort, then least recent assignment. Nothing is marked done automatically.

Mark a chore done from the Today board and undo a mistaken check-in. Request a handoff; the other person can accept or decline on the same device. Review chronological history and completed effort per profile over a calendar week, month, year, or all time. Uncompleted work never counts as completed effort.

Everything stays on your device. There is no sign-up, advertising, analytics, or location, camera, or contacts access. Confirm before exporting an unencrypted JSON backup or replacing your current board from one. Preview and share board text with profile names hidden by default; chore titles remain visible, so check before sharing. Use the system share sheet to print if your device supports printing.

Chore Wheel is built for adults sharing a home who want a clear, shared answer to "whose turn is it" without points, allowances, or leaderboard pressure.

Chore Wheel is an iPhone app. It is a household coordination tool, not a medical, safety, or childcare service.

This listing describes the planned app. Features arrive through the public development backlog; only capabilities present in the shipped build are available in your download.
